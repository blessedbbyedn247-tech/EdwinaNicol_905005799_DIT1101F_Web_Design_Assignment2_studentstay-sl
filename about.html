Who We Are

StudentStay SL is a student accommodation platform designed to make it easier for students to find safe, affordable and reliable accommodation near their university and other important locations.

We understand the challenges students face when searching for accommodation. That is why we built a user-friendly website that brings together all the information you need in one place, connecting students across Sierra Leone with the best living options.
